# Public Free Suffix: Free Domain Names For Everyone

**Public Free Suffix** is a non-profit free subdomain service, built and continuously operated by the community, for the community. It exists for developers, students, technicians, and personal users who need a domain for a blog, a portfolio, a lab, or a weekend experiment.

## How to apply

Applications are handled entirely through the Nssnap Community web console: no pull requests, and no waiting on email verification.

1. **Check availability** for the name you want in the [web console](https://nssnap.com/domains).
2. **Create an account in the [web console](https://nssnap.com/domains/register)** and verify your GitHub account. Your GitHub account must be at least 2 years old.
3. **Register the domain and resolve it on Nssnap DNS.** Every domain includes Nssnap DNS, our global resolution service: A, AAAA, MX, NS, CAA, TXT, CNAME, ALIAS, HTTPS, and more record types are supported. No third-party DNS setup needed.

## Supported suffixes

```text
bg5.net
is-a.si
nastu.net
tun.re
us.mw
pfsdns.org
```

## The fine print

- Domain names must be a single label under the suffix (e.g. `myname.is-a.si`, not `my.name.is-a.si`), and must not hit the [reserved words list](reserved_words.txt).
- By registering you agree to the [Registration and Use Agreement](agreements/registration-and-use-agreement-sokg.md), the [Acceptable Use Policy](agreements/acceptable-use-policy.md), and the [Privacy Policy](agreements/privacy-policy.md).
- Please deploy something within **30 days**. Domains held for 30 consecutive days without any content are reclaimed, so resources go to people who actually use them.

## Report abuse

If you find a domain being used for phishing, fraud, gambling, malware, or anything else that violates the Acceptable Use Policy, please tell us:

- Online: [nssnap.com/report](https://nssnap.com/report)
- Email: [abuse-report@publicfreesuffix.org](mailto:abuse-report@publicfreesuffix.org)

## Where did the pull-request flow go?

Between 2025 and 2026, registrations were handled through pull requests in this repository. That flow has been retired; the web console is faster and friendlier for everyone. If you applied through a pull request in the past, sign in to the [web console](https://nssnap.com/domains) with your original Whois email address and your domain will be waiting for you.

Questions, ideas, or just want to say hi? Post on the community forum with the **#PFS** tag. We read everything.
